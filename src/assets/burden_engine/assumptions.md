- **Shaft efficiency** is either Fixed-ω (held at the calibration, the Rist baseline; ASM-01; the default) or STRATA-ω (ASM-18, GAS-13..25, STRATA 1.5): the STRATA-Flow gas-flow network's ring counter-current description of the thermal reserve zone (size of each ferrous, coke layers, fuels, oxygen, nut coke), solved for every plan - one network solve per optimiser iteration and three per reported plan, about 1 s each, no surrogate. Plant-data scales: pellet 1.42 ± 0.45, lump ore 0.92 ± 0.46, other operation 0.80 ± 0.28; nut-coke term; no production channel (production acts through the balance; the channel was not replicated on 2023-24 data, GAS-16) and no moisture term (the day's Open-Meteo moisture acts through the balance, GAS-19). Tested out of sample on 175 days of 2026 (burnt-coke RMSE 5.99 against 6.39 kg/tHM for Fixed-ω, skill +0.12 day, +0.22 7-day; GAS-20) and with frozen parameters on 411 days of 2023-24 (RMSE 8.00 against 8.23 kg/tHM; GAS-25). Each plan carries σ(coke), a certainty class (High / Medium / Low / Investigate), its critical assumptions and the network solves behind it; the calibration's own error (about ±2 kg/tHM) and the day-to-day spread (about ±7 kg/tHM) are reported apart.
- **Reducibility and RDI** are the Evonith laboratory tests as given (GAS-21, DATA-13: pellet 82, Lloyds CLO 84, Geomin 79, NMDC ROM 61, Donimalai 78 % RI; Gomti not tested). The ore RDIs act in the network as shaft fines. The link from RI to reduction rate is calibrated on the plant data: the measured RI differences show no effect on the plant's shaft efficiency, particle size does (RI exponent 0, size exponent 1.0; both are critical assumptions of every plan).
- **Oversize**: the Lloyds fines oversize (ore_6) reduces at the Lloyds CLO size (no size credit, GAS-24); the Donimalai oversize return (ore_11) is Donimalai.
- **Investigate tier**: pellet above 25 % of ferrous or σ(coke) above 8 kg/tHM is beyond common practice; such plans are excluded by default and are trial proposals only.
- **Blast moisture** is the day's Open-Meteo relative humidity at Wardha mapped to 7-21 g/Nm³ (DATA-11; validated on the top-gas H₂, DATA-16). It acts on coke through the balance; STRATA 1.5 has no separate moisture term (GAS-19 retired). **PCI** defaults to the live tag's 1-h mean (else the regime window's median) and **nut coke** to 70 kg/tHM, unless overridden; both act through the balance, nut coke also through STRATA-ω (GAS-23).
- **Burden-resistance rules** (FLOW-02..07) were fitted on pellet up to 16.6 % and Gomti up to 11.6 % of ferrous; beyond that the page warns that it is extrapolating.
- **Process limits** default to the Jun-Oct 2026 operating envelope, not equipment ratings. Oxygen and stove gas have no price.
- **Not limited:** phosphorus, alkalis, zinc, sulphur; sinter-plant cost of a basicity change.
- **Slag** is the Blend Mix Optimiser's ledger on the plan's burden: hot-metal Si, S and C held for the slag side (page 9's values, Si 0.60 %; the coke balance keeps its thermal Si), top-gas dust as the plant burden sheet's row (530 kg per charge, CaO 5.63 %, MgO 3.57 %, held constant over the blend's charges per tHM; editable on page 11 and the SPA as operator inputs), TiO₂ left out of the slag total (as the UAT page 9), alkalis 80 % to slag, sulphur less the pig iron's and 10 % to gas, FeO from 0.1 % of the Fe charged less the dust's.
- Steady day average: no transition between blends; heat losses (GJ/h) stay at the calibration.
